Sky Fighter - Privacy Policy

Developed by NextGenX Studio

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sky Fighter, a 2D arcade shooting game developed and published by NextGenX Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, or use Sky Fighter.

By using the game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information and Game Progress

Sky Fighter stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • Game scores and high scores.
  • Virtual coin balances.
  • Game progress and preferences.
  • Music and sound settings.

This information is used to provide gameplay features and maintain your game experience. We do not require users to create an account to play the game.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Sky Fighter uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, which may include:

  • IP address.
  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and improving advertising services. The information collected and its use may depend on your device, privacy settings, and applicable consent requirements.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies. For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

4. In-App Purchases

Sky Fighter offers optional in-app purchases, including virtual coins and additional game features.

All purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment details, such as credit or debit card information, are handled by Google Play and its payment providers. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details.

Google Play may process transaction information according to its own privacy and payment policies. Virtual items purchased in the game are intended for use within Sky Fighter and have no real-world monetary value.

5. Children's Privacy

Sky Fighter is intended for users aged 13 and older. The game is not directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

Advertising services may apply their own age-related restrictions and privacy protections in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the game. Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Information processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is handled according to their respective privacy and data retention policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Certain information may be processed or shared by third-party services necessary to provide advertising and in-app purchases, including Google AdMob and Google Play Billing.

These services handle information under their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage advertising privacy and personalization preferences through the settings available on your Android device and your Google account, where supported.

You can remove locally stored game progress by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Please note that this may delete your locally saved scores and coin balance.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the game, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
