Sky Fighter - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sky Fighter, a 2D arcade shooting game, handles information when you use the app.

Information and Game Progress

The game may store game progress, such as scores and coin balances, locally on your device. This information is used to provide game features and maintain your progress.

Data Collection and Sharing

The information collected or shared depends on the app features and third-party services included in the version you use. This policy must be updated if the app uses analytics, advertising, online services, or other tools that collect or share data.

In-App Purchases

If in-app purchases are enabled, purchases are processed through Google Play. Google Play may process transaction information under its own privacy terms. The app's final purchase and data-handling practices should be described here accurately before release.

Children's Privacy

The app's target audience and any data practices relating to children must be described in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

Security and Data Retention

Game progress stored locally may remain on your device until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Any additional data retention and security practices depend on the services used by the app.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
